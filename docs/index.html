The problem

A family can slip out of a memory, and he out of theirs.

“Yaadain keeps the family from disappearing from the elder’s memory, and keeps the elder from disappearing from the family.”

The same questions, again and againHe asks what day it is, who is here. Each time, the family answers as if it were the first.
Faces he cannot placeSomeone is in the room and he cannot tell who they are to him.
A walk that becomes a worryWhen he is outside, the family needs to know, and he needs to know who is coming.
Few apps are made for this homeUrdu-speaking joint families, voice-first, are a population Western apps do not serve.
The idea

A living family tree, in the family’s own voice.

Every relative is three things, kept together and kept on the device. Yaadain plays only what the family actually recorded. It never generates speech or invents a memory.

1
A faceLabelled from his point of view: “your son, Bilal”.
2
Their real voiceShort hellos and answers, recorded by the family.
3
Their relationship to the elderPrecise kinship, spoken gently; a relative who has passed has a sepia ring.

The elder’s phone

Entirely Urdu. Never a quiz.

Noto Nastaliq, right to left, Urdu digits. He asks from six fixed questions, answered in the family’s own words and voices, and every ask is logged for the weekly report.

Design principles

Built for his hands and his eyes.

56px

Touch targets

Every target is 56px or larger. Main actions are 64 to 80px.

20px

Urdu, first

No Urdu text is smaller than 20px, set in Noto Nastaliq, right to left.

1

Language per screen

Elder screens are all Urdu, family screens all English. An automated script-mixing check enforces it.

1

Clay action

One warm clay colour marks the single most important action on a screen.

Calm

Same back, same home

Every screen has the same back and home buttons. Red is kept for a true emergency.

Privacy and offline-first

He agreed. It stays on his phone.

Consent firstExplained to him in Urdu, then recorded, with a review date.
Offline-firstFaces, voices and relationships live on the phone. No network or account needed.
No location trailOnly his latest position. A memory companion is not a surveillance device.
No real patient dataThe demo family is fictional and uses consenting stand-ins.
Firebase is optionalSharing between phones is written, and switched off in the demo build.
What we did not buildNo voice cloning, no reality correction, no agitation-detection ML.
